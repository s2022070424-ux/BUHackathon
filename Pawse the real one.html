Feature 01 / 06 • Streamline Studying

AI Syllabus-to-Sprint Deconstructor & Custom Goal Details

Students procrastinate when faced with a vague, overwhelming syllabus. Pawse ingests uploaded syllabus PDFs, rubric photos, or raw text alongside your timeframe and daily hours goal—automatically breaking chapters into bite-sized 20–30 minute study sprints with custom task descriptions and practice questions.

5-Minute Anti-Procrastination Ramp-Up & Click-to-Edit Timer

Starting a 2-hour study block feels daunting, so students don't start at all. Our Preset Time 1 (5m Ramp-Up) lowers the activation energy to just 300 seconds. Users can also switch cleanly between dedicated Focus Mode and Break Mode tabs or click directly on the circular timer to type any custom minute duration.

Tunable Lofi Music & Binaural Frequency Synthesizer

Feature 04 / 06 • Wellness & Cortisol Reset

10-Minute Automatic Water Alert & 5s-Prep Breathing Circle

Every 10 minutes during an active Focus Mode timer, Pawse plays a gentle water-droplet chime and slides out a hydration reminder (+5 Pet Tokens). When stress rises, opening Mental Break Caltrops gives the user a 5-second preparation countdown before guiding them through Box (4-4-4-4) or 4-7-8 breathing to lower heart rate and cortisol.

Diminishing-Reward Topic Quizzes & 12-Month Task Calendar

To prevent random guessing, each AI-generated multiple-choice question starts at +25 Tokens and reduces the reward on each wrong choice (+25 → +15 → +5 → 0 Tokens), locking once claimed so rewards can never be farmed twice.
